How to write a line

M visoka nota · m niska nota
MMM one long note, length = how many letters
Ta a syllable, one note · TaTaTa three notes
- short · – medium · — long
^ pojacan ton, the accented apex
__ same length as —, but drugi glas
space a rest · . .. .... beat hits
In a drums lane: K kick S snare H hat T tom C crash

Type a lane, then tap root on that lane to find its pitch by ear. Tap any bar in the preview to tune that one note on its own.

Real instruments. Piano, violin, strings, bass and two human voices are actual recordings, so each one downloads the first time you pick it. A few seconds on wifi, then it is instant forever. Until it lands you get a rough stand-in sound rather than silence. Load all real sounds in the menu warms everything at once.

Layers. Tap new layer and everything you already recorded plays back as a guide while you play on top, so parts stack instead of queueing. Four hihat ticks count you in. Each layer has loop to make it repeat, on/off to mute it, and one looping layer is the anchor - every other repeat falls in line with it so they can never drift apart. Two layers on the same instrument is just two of that instrument playing at once.

Pluck and Saw are the two takes on your TaTa sound: a short bright hit that collapses straight away, with a small tail so silence never lands before the next one. Saw sweeps darker as it falls. Tell me which is closer. Hum and Aah are recorded voices.

Dials

drugi glas, semitones below prvi
niska m, semitones below M
accent ^, semitones above

Default drugi glas is a second - tight enough that the two voices fuse and ring rather than reading as separate notes. Slide it until your ear says yes, it sticks from then on.
